Questions

Is it safe?

QuietTV only switches apps off (never deletes them) and never touches what the TV needs: Google Play, Settings, the keyboard, Bluetooth, HDMI inputs, or the TV's own home screen and setup app. Every change is recorded on the TV and can be undone with one tap in the phone app, or sh quiettv.sh undo.

Which TVs does it work on?

Android TV and Google TV: TVs from Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips, Xiaomi, Acer and others, plus Chromecast with Google TV and Nvidia Shield. Not Roku, Samsung, LG or Fire TV's home screen. It's been tested on an Acer Google TV (Android 14). Please tell us how it goes on yours.

I have an iPhone

The phone app is Android only for now. Use a computer (Mac, Windows or Linux) instead; it takes about the same time.

Why does it need "developer mode"?

That's the only way an app on another device is allowed to switch off TV apps and set up the Home button. Your TV asks you to approve each phone or computer once, and you can remove that approval anytime in Developer options.

What's Dev mode?

For developers: your public GitHub contribution graph on the home screen, with your yearly total and streaks. Turn it on from the ✦ QuietTV tile on the TV (or sh quiettv.sh dev <TV_IP> <github-name>). It's the only time QuietTV goes online, and then only to github.com. No account or token needed.

How do I undo everything?

In QuietTV Setup: "See what QuietTV changed", then "Undo everything". With a computer: sh quiettv.sh undo <TV_IP>. Your TV goes back exactly to how it was.
